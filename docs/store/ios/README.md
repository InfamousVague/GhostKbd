# Ghost Keyboard for iOS: what is here

Everything needed to put the iOS app on the App Store. The App IDs, the App Group and the two App Store profiles are registered
(2026-11-10); nothing has been uploaded and the app record does not exist yet.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `SUBMIT.md` | The steps, in order: what to register, how to archive and upload, what to fill in. |
| `metadata.md` | Name, subtitle, description, keywords, what's new, category, age rating. No third-party names. |
| `screenshots.md`, `screenshots/` | The screenshots taken (iPhone 6.9", iPad 13"), the plan they follow, and what must not be in them. |
| `privacy-policy.md` | The privacy policy's text, to host at the Privacy Policy URL. |
| `app-privacy.md` | Answers for App Store Connect's App Privacy questionnaire, with the reasoning. |
| `review-notes.md` | The note for App Review: why Full Access, how to test, what each permission is for. |

The technical side (architecture, what is in v1, what is deferred, memory) is `docs/IOS.md`.
