## Description

    Ghost Keyboard types like the keyboard you know, with the things people who write code reach for a tap away.

    A PAGE OF CODE
    Beside the space bar is the { } key. It opens a page of what code is made of: brackets and quotes come in pairs with the cursor already inside, and every operator holds its family (= gives you ==, ===, != and the rest). Tab, backticks, arrows and snippets are one press.

    TYPING THAT KEEPS UP
    • Autocorrect in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese, several at once. It knows the words of a programmer's day (async, kubectl, localhost) and leaves camelCase and code alone.
    • Swipe typing: glide across the letters to spell a word.
    • Adaptive keys learn where your thumbs actually land and move each key's touch area to suit. Everything it learns stays on your device.
    • Slide along the space bar to move the cursor, up or down for lines, and left from delete to take whole words.
    • Undo and redo, arrow keys, Markdown and terminal keys in the row above the letters.

    YOUR LOOK
    Light, dark, preset themes, a theme of your own from five colours, and a mechanical-keycap look. A backlight makes the legends glow as you type: reactive, ripple, heatmap, rainbow, starlight or breathe.

    AN AI ASSISTANT, ON YOUR TERMS (OPTIONAL)
    Type what you want between double angle brackets in any app and the answer is written in its place. Select text and have it rewritten shorter, more formal, or in another language. Teach it how you write and it answers in your voice.
    • You bring your own API key from the AI provider you choose. Requests go straight from your device to that provider. We run no servers and receive nothing.
    • Nothing is sent unless you use one of these features, and only after you have read and agreed to what is sent.
    • The keyboard works fully without it.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
    • What you type is not sent anywhere by ordinary typing.
    • Without Allow Full Access the keyboard still types, corrects and themes. Full Access is only needed for the AI assistant, clipboard history, haptic feedback, and keeping your settings in step with the app.
