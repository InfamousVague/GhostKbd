Ghost Keyboard: Support

For Ghost Keyboard on iPhone and iPad.

Getting started

  • Add the keyboard: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard… › Ghost Keyboard. The Ghost Keyboard app's Setup page opens the right place for you.
  • Switch to it: hold the globe key under any keyboard and pick Ghost Keyboard.
  • Allow Full Access is optional. Without it the keyboard types, corrects and themes; with it, it can use the AI features you turn on, clipboard history, haptics, and keep its settings in step with the app. Nothing you type is sent anywhere by ordinary typing.

Common questions

  • The AI features say they are off. They need Allow Full Access, an API key from the AI provider you choose (in the app's AI settings), and your agreement on the app's consent screen.
  • Why does iOS show its own keyboard sometimes? iOS uses its own keyboard for passwords and in some apps.
  • Dictation and emoji. iOS doesn't let a keyboard use the microphone; the dictation key under the keyboard still works. The emoji keyboard is one tap of the globe away.
  • The globe and microphone under the keyboard are drawn by iOS. The globe goes when Ghost Keyboard is your only keyboard; the microphone goes when Enable Dictation is off in Settings › General › Keyboard.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/InfamousVague/GhostKbd. Please say which iPhone or iPad and which iOS version you use.

Privacy
